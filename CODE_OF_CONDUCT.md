# Code of Conduct

This is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part, whether in issues, discussions, pull requests or by sending files, is expected to follow a few simple rules.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intentions and ask when something is unclear.
- Criticise ideas and results, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and people with less experience; explain instead of belittling.
- Stay on topic. Keep discussions about the project, the cameras and the file formats.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination or hateful language of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information (names, addresses, camera serial numbers, personal text in files) without their consent.
- Sharing files or material that you are not allowed to share, such as paid look packs or files of other people.
- Spam, advertising and repeated off-topic posts.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, comments, pull requests, e-mail about the project) and to anyone who represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and files that do not follow this code of conduct, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it. If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, please report it by e-mail to sonyconfigeditor@gmail.com. Reports are treated confidentially.

This is the maintainers' own short text; the project may adopt a longer standard code of conduct later.
